# PRD 1 — FOCUS CLOCK: WAY OF LIFE (SADAR)

**Status:** Approved Baseline  
**Type:** Companion app / integrated module of Focus Clock  
**Primary Goal:** Help users consciously fulfill their day through repeated meaningful actions.

---

## 1. Product Definition

**Sadar** is a multiplatform companion application connected seamlessly to **Focus Clock**.

It answers one simple question:
> **“Did I live today in a way I can be proud of?”**

The app turns meaningful daily actions into visible, accumulated evidence of a life being lived intentionally.

Examples:
* French — 20 min
* Quranic Arabic — 15 min
* Programming — 60 min
* Guitar — 20 min
* Exercise — 30 min
* Reading — 20 min
* Prayer / reflection
* Writing
* Any personally meaningful habit

The purpose is **not maximizing streaks**.
The purpose is **awareness → action → repetition → a fulfilled day**.

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

### 2.1 Fulfill Your Day
A day does not need to be perfect. A good day is a day where you consciously did what mattered to you.

### 2.2 Atomic Habits Integration (~33%)
* Make it obvious: See what matters today.
* Make it easy: 1-tap recording (<5 seconds).
* Make it satisfying: Visual accumulated evidence of repeated action.

### 2.3 Conscious Awareness
> *"Until you make the unconscious conscious, it will direct your life and you will call it fate."* — Carl Jung

### 2.4 Connected, Not Coupled
Sadar and Focus Clock operate independently and integrate through a clean contract.
